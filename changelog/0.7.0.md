# 0.7.0 - 2026-11-25

Videos from social feeds, a new download menu and a progress card on the page you download from.

## Features

- **Social video sites:** download videos, reels and short videos straight from the feed, every available size up to 4K, named after the post's caption and author, whether you are logged in or not.
- **Live broadcasts and replays:** recorded live streams download in every quality they were broadcast in.
- **The button is there at once:** the download button appears on a video right away; its sizes load only when you click it, so scrolling stays smooth.
- **New video menu:** one row per real frame size with its estimated file size, a subtitle picker, Reload to look again, and Close.
- **Confirm on the page:** the save dialog can now open on the page itself, so you choose the folder and name without leaving the browser.
- **Progress card:** the website you started a download from shows its progress in a small card you can move; pause, resume, open the file or its folder from there.
- **All downloads in the browser:** a full download list in a browser tab or the side panel, with filters by file type, details, and buttons to open a finished file or its folder.
- **Ads too:** video ads in a feed can be downloaded like any other video.

## Fixes

- Some feeds showed "Couldn't find download links" for every video; they now list all sizes.
- Videos played by some sites' newer players got no download button.
- In a feed, the menu could show the video of a neighbouring post instead of the one you clicked.
- The browser's download list names the website a download came from instead of the server that stored the file, and long names no longer widen its side bar.
- Download buttons no longer appear on hidden or empty players, such as previews in closed menus.
- Buttons on short videos no longer vanish while the video plays or when you scroll through a grid.
- Reload in the video menu no longer lists the same video twice.
- The app no longer crashes when its window opens from the tray, and uses less memory while it waits there.
