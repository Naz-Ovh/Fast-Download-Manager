# 0.8.1 - 2026-11-26

Torrents: download and share `.torrent` files and magnet links, in the app and straight from your browser.

## Features

- **Torrents:** open a `.torrent` file or a magnet link from the app, by drag and drop, from the command line, from Windows or from the browser; pick the files you want and where to save them.
- **Fast and complete:** peers are found through trackers, the DHT network, peer exchange and your local network; every piece is checked, downloads resume after a restart, and your router's port is opened automatically.
- **Preview while downloading:** download a torrent in order to watch a video before it finishes.
- **Seeding:** finished torrents are shared until a share ratio or a time limit you choose; a new **Seed after downloading** switch turns seeding off entirely, in the app and in the browser extension's settings.
- **Torrent details:** a panel under the download list shows speed graphs, pieces, files, peers and trackers, with downloaded and uploaded amounts for all time and for this session.
- **Torrents in the browser:** clicking a magnet link or a `.torrent` download shows the files on the page before the app starts it; a small card shows the `.torrent` file's progress, with **Just download the file** to save it as a normal download.
- **All downloads in the browser** lists torrents too, with filters, a resizable details panel, piece map and graphs; live download and upload speeds now stand out on every card.
- **Newest first:** the download list now shows the latest download at the top.
- **Speed limits and encryption** for torrents, globally and per torrent, and a firewall rule set up by the installer.

## Fixes

- The download list scrolls smoothly, even with many downloads, and the app uses less CPU while you move the mouse over it.
- Quitting the app never hangs; it saves its state and closes within seconds.
- The browser's download list no longer asks you to update the desktop app when it is already up to date.
- The download button on a video no longer adds hidden elements to the page when it is not shown.
- Download progress in the browser moves smoothly and uses less CPU.
- An error in the browser's extension log ("Invalid keyframe value") no longer appears while a video's links are scanned.
