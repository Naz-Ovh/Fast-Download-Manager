---
title: Roadmap
description: What is coming next to Fast Download Manager, grouped by area.
---

These features are planned. They are listed by area, not by date.

## Downloads

- **Queues and scheduler:** start and stop times, how many files at once, and what to do when done (shut down,
  sleep or exit).
- **Download all links:** a window to pick from the links the extension collected, and batch downloads from
  address patterns on the clipboard.
- **Site grabber:** fetch a whole site, or every file of a type, by following its links.
- **Clipboard monitoring:** offer a download when you copy a link to a file.
- **FTP** downloads.
- **Proxy and SOCKS:** SOCKS proxies, automatic proxy scripts, per-site settings.
- **Site logins:** a list of user names and passwords per site, and more sign-in methods.
- **Import and export** of download lists.
- **A progress window per download:** speed, connections, the chunk map, pause and the speed limit at a glance.
- **The Options window** for every setting, and a speed limit you set yourself.
- **Duplicate choices:** overwrite or resume instead of always saving a numbered copy.
- **Sites with bot protection:** a browser-grade connection so these sites accept the app.

## Video

- **More sites with their own players.**
- **Convert to MP4** for TS streams.
- **HDS** (`.f4m`) streams.

## Platforms and install

- **Windows installer** (`.msi`): just for you or for everyone, starts with Windows.
- **Linux:** `.deb`, `.rpm`, `.pacman`, AppImage and a one-line install command, with a tray icon on KDE and
  GNOME.
- **macOS:** a `.dmg`.
- **Browser stores:** Chrome Web Store, Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons, so the extension installs
  with one click.

## Beyond

- **BitTorrent and magnet links:** download and seed torrents, with trackers, DHT and magnet links, in the same
  list as your other downloads.
